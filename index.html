Cyber Security

Security is not a layer. Every layer has an attack surface, and the weakest one sets your real posture.

Cyber security is a property of every layer, not a place in the stack. The firewall is worth little if the server is unpatched, and a hardened server is worth little if the login form leaks. We work the whole stack, so the hardening is done at the depth the risk actually lives — and the backups are tested rather than assumed.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?+

Static brochure sites start from £999, dynamic/CMS sites from £1,999, and Shopify stores from £2,999. You get a fixed quote after a free consultation — no surprises.

How long does it take?+

Most websites launch within 2–4 weeks of kick-off, depending on size and how quickly content is ready.

Do you work with businesses like mine?+

Yes — we specialise in small businesses and startups, from local services to online stores and tech startups. We're a remote-first studio and we work with clients anywhere in the world, around your hours rather than ours.

Can you work with me from another country?+

Yes. Everything we do runs remotely — calls, updates, handover and support — so where you are makes no difference to the work. We agree response times and meeting hours that fit your time zone before we start. Our prices are quoted and invoiced in GBP (£), and every price on the site is shown that way. Pick your own currency from the header and an approximate conversion appears under each figure, so you can judge the cost without doing the maths — the sterling amount is still the one that goes in the contract, and it is confirmed in writing before any work begins.

What happens after launch?+

Every project includes 30 days' support. After that you can take a care plan for ongoing updates, monitoring, and changes.

Can you help me get more customers, not just a website?+

Absolutely. Alongside web development we run SEO, social, paid ads, and email marketing — month-to-month, no lock-in.
