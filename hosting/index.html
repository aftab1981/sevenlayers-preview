Questions

Common questions.

Is this cheaper than the big hosts?+

No, and it is not trying to be. You can host a site for £3 a month, and if price is the deciding factor that is genuinely the right answer — we will tell you so rather than talk you out of it. What £3 does not buy is somebody who already knows your site picking up the phone when it breaks at 9am on a Monday.

Do you own the servers?+

No. The infrastructure belongs to an established provider with proper data centres, redundant power and staff on site around the clock. What is ours is the management, the monitoring, the backups and the person who answers you. That is an honest description of almost every host at this size — most of them just do not say it out loud.

What is your uptime guarantee?+

We do not publish one yet, because we have not run long enough to have measured it honestly. When there is a real track record it will appear here with the data behind it. Anyone quoting you 99.99% on their first day is quoting their supplier's marketing rather than their own history.

What happens if my site goes down at 2am?+

Monitoring alerts a person, not just a dashboard. On the Managed plan we act in working hours; on Managed Plus and Portfolio we respond out of hours too. We would rather set that expectation precisely than promise 24/7 heroics and disappoint you the first time it matters.

Can I leave?+

Yes, and we will help. Your domain is registered in your name, your site and database are yours, and we will hand over a full export or migrate it to your new host. A hosting company that makes leaving difficult is telling you what it thinks of its own product.

How is this different from your care plans?+

Hosting is the floor; the care plans are the work that happens on top. Hosting keeps the site running, patched and backed up. A care plan adds the changes, the performance work and the review — the things a person does rather than a server. Plenty of clients take both, and the hosting is included once you are on a care plan.
