  • Secure configuration baselines
  • TLS & certificate management
  • Backup verification & restore testing
  • Business continuity & recovery planning
  • Security reviews of existing estates

IT managers · Business owners · Compliance leads

→ A smaller attack surface, patched estate, and a restore you have actually watched work.
