What we delivered

Everything they needed to sell three product lines at once.

01

Custom WordPress theme

A clean, fast-loading theme built to match the MK Repairz brand rather than a marketplace template bent into shape. Mobile-first, because that is where the enquiries come from.

02

One catalogue, three lines

Repairs, vapes and accessories are different purchases with different browsing habits, sold from one shop without any of them feeling bolted on. Search runs across all three.

03

Accounts, cart and checkout

Customers sign in, keep a basket and check out — plus promotional codes the team create and expire themselves, which is how the discount campaigns on the homepage actually run.

04

SEO Foundation

Meta tags, structured data, and sitemap set up from day one so the store starts ranking from launch.

05

Speed optimised

Image compression, lazy loading, caching and a lean plugin set — a WordPress site is only as fast as what you leave out of it.

06

Training & handover

Full walkthrough so the team edit services, prices and content themselves. Documented and handed over — no agency dependency, nothing held hostage.
