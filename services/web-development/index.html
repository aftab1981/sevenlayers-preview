Questions

Common questions about web development.

How long does a website take to build?+

Static sites: 1–2 weeks. Dynamic sites with CMS: 2–4 weeks. Shopify stores: 3–5 weeks. Timelines depend on how quickly you provide content and feedback.

Do I need to provide content?+

We'll guide you on what content you need. We can also write copy for your site for an additional fee. We handle all the design and development.

Will I be able to update the website myself?+

Yes for dynamic sites and Shopify — we train you to make updates. Static sites require us to make changes, though this is quick and low cost.

What platform do you build on?+

We recommend WordPress for most content-driven sites, Shopify for ecommerce, and Next.js for performance-critical or custom builds. We'll recommend the best fit on your consultation call.

What happens after launch?+

30 days of free support for bug fixes. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance plans. We also offer ongoing digital marketing to keep growing your traffic.
