The challenges

What holds jewellery & accessories back online.

01

Templates flatten the pieces

A generic grid makes a hand-set kundan piece look like a phone case. Jewellery needs layouts built around photography, not around a product feed.

02

Buyers shop by occasion

Nobody wakes up needing a chandbali. They need something for a wedding, or for every day. Browsing by occasion sells far more than browsing by category alone.

03

Trust decides the sale

Imitation, plated, zircon, fine — a buyer needs to know exactly what they are getting. Stores that say it plainly convert better than stores that stay vague and hope.

04

Bridal is a set, not a product

Necklace, jhumka, tikka and ring sell together, often after a conversation. A store that cannot merchandise a set or start that conversation loses the highest-value order it has.

FAQ

Jewellery & Accessories — common questions.

Can you build a store for imitation or artificial jewellery?+

Yes — that is exactly what we designed ZEVAR for. The important part is saying plainly what a piece is: gold-tone or antique plating, zircon rather than diamond, faux pearl, skin-friendly base. Buyers reward stores that are specific and distrust stores that are vague.

Do you support Cash on Delivery and WhatsApp ordering?+

Yes. In several markets most jewellery orders are paid at the door and half of them start in a WhatsApp conversation. We build the checkout around how your customers actually pay rather than assuming a card-first flow.

We do not have professional product photography yet. Can we still start?+

Yes. We build the store so photography drops into it later without a redesign, and we will tell you honestly what the shots need to look like. Jewellery is one of the few sectors where photography, not copy, decides whether the store sells.

Can the same design be adapted for our brand?+

Yes. The storefront we designed is a pattern, not a one-off: catalogue, occasion browsing, set merchandising and the checkout all carry over. Rebuilding it around your brand, your pieces and your market is a fraction of designing one from scratch.

Shopify or a custom build?+

Shopify for most stores — it handles stock, orders and payments so you do not have to. Custom where the merchandising is unusual enough that a template fights you. We will say which one your case needs before quoting, and we will say so if the cheaper answer is the right one.
